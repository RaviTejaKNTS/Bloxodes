GTA V world-space location coordinate research
Source: https://github.com/rootBrz/collectibles-on-map-gta-5/blob/main/src/collectibles/coords.h
Cross-check: https://github.com/danharper/GTAV/blob/master/locations.json
Zone cross-check: https://github.com/VIRUXE/gtav-coordinate-lookup/blob/main/data/zones.json
The source coordinate arrays were matched to Bloxodes guide item numbers by landmark,
route description, geographic zone and spatial position. The map data records game
x/y positions; z (height) is not needed for a 2D marker.
