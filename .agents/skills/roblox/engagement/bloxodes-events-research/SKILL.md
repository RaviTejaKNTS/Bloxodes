# Bloxodes Events Research

You're researching one approved events page so the writer can explain how events work in this game without going stale. You hand back a `brief.md`. You don't write `final.json`.

## Output

```text
tmp/content-workspace/<game-slug>/events/<game-slug>/brief.md
```

## What the brief includes

- the existing Bloxodes events page and timeline rows
- the exact game identity and universe ID
- the event source path
- whether timeline rows come from an approved importer
- the evergreen value of the page
- facts to use
- facts to avoid
- open gaps or risks

## Writing the brief for the writer

The page copy has to stay evergreen, so give the writer facts that won't expire. Read `.agents/skills/bloxodes-voice/SKILL.md` first.

- **Plain player language.** "New events usually drop with weekend updates and bring a limited shop," not "event cadence correlates with update releases."
- **Name the reader's real question:** when do events happen, what do they get, and how do they join?
- **Hooks worth using:** a lasting quirk of how events work in this game, like a reward that never comes back or a join step players often miss.
- **Keep sources and doubts private.** They go in your evidence notes or open gaps. If a pattern is shaky, say so once in plain words so the writer can add one short caveat or leave it out.
- **No research jargon in the facts to use.** Words like importer, timeline rows and source path stay in your notes.
- Keep live event dates, statuses, reward timelines and "current event" claims out of the facts to use. The importer and timeline data own those.
