## One-sentence editorial thesis

State the most important human story without methodology language.

## Why this is the lead

Explain why the observation is surprising, defensible, and meaningful at its scale. Name competing leads that were considered and why they are secondary.

## Recommended story flow

### Opening

Describe the hook, exact facts to use, and first chart placement.

### Main section 1

Describe the broader genre/event pattern and evidence.

### Main section 2

Describe climbers, cool-downs, contrasts, and platform/community ending.

Keep the finished page to no more than three H2 sections.

## Exact game evidence

Create one subsection per serious story candidate:

- game name, universe ID, Bloxodes slug, genre, creation date;
- usable daily average;
- observed low and high dates/values;
- median same-weekday weekly change;
- share of same-weekday comparisons that rose;
- strongest and weakest seven-day averages;
- weekday pattern;
- relevant Roblox events and update dates;
- safe interpretation;
- interpretations that the evidence does not support;
- Bloxodes stats link and external corroborating sources.

## Genre evidence

Include a compact table:

| Genre | Stable games | Typical same-weekday change | Share that rose | Combined daily average | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Explain scale, persistence, internal rotation, and any thin genre that should be omitted.

## Cool-down evidence

Use full-period or rolling paths:

| Game | Monthly daily average | Typical weekly change | Strongest 7-day avg | Weakest 7-day avg | Safe wording |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Name a control or counterexample when it prevents an overbroad genre claim.

## Platform, news, and community context

For every candidate item:

- event date;
- primary or reputable source URL;
- plain-language significance;
- exact claim allowed;
- attribution required;
- connection to game data, if any;
- causal claim explicitly forbidden.

Separate:

- official Roblox changes;
- major community or creator developments;
- legal/safety/business news;
- individual-game fandom or sentiment signals.

## Chart specifications

For every proposed figure:

- title;
- chart type;
- exact games/genres;
- date range;
- field or calculation;
- normalization/index explanation;
- event markers and source;
- caption;
- accessibility treatment;
- claim the chart supports;
- misleading interpretation to avoid.

Limit the report to four figures.

## Feature image specification

- short social headline, manually split into one to three lines;
- plain `Roblox <Month> Stats Report` context line;
- one meaningful lead metric in plain language;
- exact report series used for the sparkline;
- accessible alt text;
- public PNG path.

The feature image must use the report's approved lead observation. Do not introduce a new fact or visual claim only for social sharing.

## Public writing and design guardrails

Copy the relevant constraints from `references/editorial-standard.md` and add month-specific warnings.

## Facts to avoid

List rejected metrics, anomalies, unverified peaks, weak sources, causal claims, and tempting but misleading comparisons.

## Sources used

Group by:

- Bloxodes/Roblox data;
- Roblox primary sources;
- official game sources;
- independent reporting;
- community context.

Put each URL beside the exact fact it supports.

## Sources found but not used

Name irrelevant, duplicative, thin, blocked, or unreliable sources and why they were excluded.

## Limitations

Cover:

- excluded dates or incomplete samples;
- selected-game scope;
- simultaneous players versus unique people;
- private retention/recommendation/revenue data not observed;
- event timing not proving causation;
- incomplete community-sentiment measurement;
- genre classification limits.

## Suggested public endnote

Write one short paragraph only.

## Definition of success

State the two or three things a casual reader should remember after reading the page.
