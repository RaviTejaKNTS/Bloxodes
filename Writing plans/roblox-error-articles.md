# Roblox Error Articles — Writing Plan

A clean content map for Roblox error / "how to fix" articles. We are starting fresh: the 14 old error articles (only 3 published) are considered bad and will be deleted/rewritten. Build top-down by traffic.

**Approach:** every article is a focused "how to fix" page in simple language, with steps, causes, internal links, and links to Roblox/Studio/Support where useful. Group the pillar page early so each new article links into it.

**Status key:** `[ ]` not started · `[~]` drafting · `[r]` in review · `[x]` published

**Totals:** ~11 generic-fix articles + ~40 code-specific articles + 1 pillar ≈ 50+ rankable pages.

## Tier 1 — Named errors & generic fixes (biggest traffic)
Not numbered codes — these are what people actually type, and the volume dwarfs most individual codes.
